The Best AirDrop Alternative That Works on Every Device

Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

Why AirDrop isn't enough

AirDrop is Apple-only. As soon as a Windows PC, Android phone or Linux laptop joins the mix, you're back to email, WhatsApp or a cable.

What a real alternative needs

Cross-OS support, no account, no install, near-instant transfer, and a self-destruct timer so nothing lingers.

Copy Cloud checks all boxes

Runs in any browser on any OS. No sign-up. Drop a file, share a 6-character code (or QR), retrieve on the other device. Clips auto-delete after 24 hours.

Comparison

AirDrop: fast, Apple-only. Nearby Share: fast, Android + Windows only. Snapdrop / peer tools: same LAN only. Copy Cloud: any device, any network, always.

Try it once

Open copycloud.me on two devices side by side and time yourself. Most people beat AirDrop's setup dialog on the first try.
