Sharing Files from iPhone to Android in 2026

Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

Why iPhone ↔ Android is still awkward

AirDrop is Apple-only. Quick Share is Android-only. Cross-platform, you're left with cloud drives, WhatsApp or email - each with sign-in, compression or size limits.

The browser bridge: online clipboard

An online clipboard doesn't care what OS you're on. Both iPhone Safari and Android Chrome can hit copycloud.me, generate a code, and pass files or text back and forth.

Step by step

1. Open Copy Cloud on the sending phone. 2. Drop your file or paste your text. 3. Read the 6-character code aloud, or scan the QR with the other phone's camera. 4. The receiving phone opens the Retrieve tab with the code pre-filled and downloads instantly.

Why this beats WhatsApp for transfers

No account, no re-compression of images, no 100 MB cap and the clip disappears after 24 hours - nothing left on any server.

Pro tip

Add Copy Cloud to your Home Screen on both phones (Share → Add to Home Screen on iOS, install icon in Chrome on Android). It launches like a native app.
