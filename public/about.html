About Copy Cloud

Copy Cloud began as a personal itch. Every day I bounced screenshots, logs and small files between a phone, a laptop and a work desktop. Email felt clumsy, chat apps compressed images, and "sign in to your Drive" was overkill for a 40 KB text file.

So I built a clipboard for the internet: open the page, drop content, share a 6-character code, retrieve on any other device. No account, no tracking, no waiting.

Copy Cloud is designed to stay tiny and honest:

  • No sign-up. Ever.
  • Everything you send is deleted after 24 hours.
  • Transfers are protected by a random code and served over HTTPS.
  • The core service will always be free.
