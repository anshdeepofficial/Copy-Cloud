What can you do with Copy Cloud?

A universal clipboard is more useful than it sounds. Here are the situations users hit every week.

Phone → laptop screenshots

Take a screenshot on your phone, drop it into Copy Cloud, then grab it on your laptop. See the phone-to-PC transfer guide for more options.

iPhone ↔ Windows transfer

AirDrop only works Apple-to-Apple. Copy Cloud gives iPhone users a fast, no-cable route to Windows; see the iPhone-to-Windows guide.

Share Wi-Fi credentials

Paste the Wi-Fi password once, hand your guest the 6-character code, and they get it on any device. Read the Wi-Fi sharing guide.

Log & error snippets

Developers paste stack traces on the server console and pick them up on their dev laptop in one step.

Design handoff

Send a PNG or PDF mock-up to a client without asking them to install Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox.

Public / lab computers

Move data through a library or classroom PC without signing into any personal account.

Interview code samples

Share a coding snippet with an interviewer without opening a repo or Gist.

Travel tickets & QR passes

Get a boarding pass PDF from your laptop onto your phone in 10 seconds.
